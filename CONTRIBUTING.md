# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve this LIMON IPTV PRO promotional repository.

This repository exists to introduce the website and direct visitors to its official website and Telegram community. It is not an application or source-code project.

## Useful contributions

- Report broken or outdated links.
- Suggest clearer and more accurate promotional wording.
- Report outdated screenshots or documentation.
- Suggest improvements to Markdown readability and accessibility.
- Flag statements that may unintentionally imply ownership of third-party content.

## Contribution guidelines

1. Keep changes focused on promotion and documentation.
2. Do not add passwords, API keys, private URLs, account details, playlists, or stream links.
3. Do not upload third-party copyrighted material unless you have permission.
4. Do not claim that a channel, broadcast, or stream is licensed or authorized unless that fact has been verified.
5. Do not make unverified promises about availability, performance, or device compatibility.
6. Keep official links pointed to the website and Telegram community listed in the README.

## Screenshots

Only submit screenshots that you are authorized to share. Identify whether each image is desktop, mobile, or a feature preview. Do not fabricate screenshots or materially misrepresent the website.

Thanks for helping keep this repository accurate and responsible.
